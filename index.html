Haven & Hue

A demonstration home décor storefront created around a calm, minimalist-luxury visual direction. The project focuses on clear product discovery, consistent branding and a straightforward shopping journey.

Project goals

Create a cohesive online storefront for a fictional home décor brand, organise products into intuitive collections, and present the brand through a polished homepage and supporting pages.

Work completed

  • Customised the Shopify Horizon theme and homepage sections.
  • Created four product collections: Decorative Vases, Home Fragrance, Soft Furnishings and Lighting.
  • Added and organised nine illustrative demo products.
  • Set up navigation, About Us, Contact Us and FAQ pages.
  • Created a matching logo, inverse logo and favicon.
  • Reviewed the storefront on desktop and mobile layouts.

Project note: Haven & Hue is a self-directed portfolio demonstration. Product names, prices and product imagery are illustrative; no supplier, inventory or live fulfilment arrangements are represented. The store remains password-protected as a Shopify development store.

FAQ

Questions, Answered

What Shopify services do you offer?

I can help with theme-based store setup and customisation, homepage layouts, product and collection organisation, navigation, basic app setup, on-page SEO basics and storefront reviews. The exact scope depends on your project.

Do I need to have my products ready?

It helps to have product names, prices, descriptions and images ready. If some details are missing, we can identify what is needed before the work begins.

Will I be able to edit the store myself?

Yes. I aim to use Shopify's theme editor and standard store tools wherever possible, so you can manage everyday content and product updates. I can also walk you through the basics at handover.

How long does a project take?

A straightforward theme-based setup may take around 1–2 weeks. The timeline depends on the number of products and pages, the features required, and how quickly content and feedback are provided.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the size and requirements of the project. Share your goals and what you need, and I’ll review the scope before providing a personalised quote.

Can you add custom-coded features?

My current services focus on Shopify's existing themes, settings and supported apps. If a project needs advanced custom development, I’ll let you know rather than promise work outside my current scope.

Do you provide product photos or supplier sourcing?

Clients provide their own product information, images and supplier details. I can help organise and upload the materials you provide, but supplier verification and fulfilment arrangements are not included.
